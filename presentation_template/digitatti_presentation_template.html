# who was born where? (CIDOC-CRM) PREFIX crm: <http://www.cidoc-crm.org/cidoc-crm/> SELECT ?name ?place WHERE { ?person a crm:E21_Person ; crm:P1_is_identified_by/crm:P190_has_symbolic_content ?name ; crm:P98i_was_born/crm:P7_took_place_at ?birthplace . ?birthplace crm:P1_is_identified_by/crm:P190_has_symbolic_content ?place . } ORDER BY ?name LIMIT 4
The highlighted line is the path: a person, a birth event, a place.

how big is your graph · the estimate

Why about 120 bytes per triple?

1 · full addresses

N-Triples writes every IRI in full, e.g. <http://www.cidoc-crm.org/cidoc-crm/P1_is_identified_by>, on every line.

2 · one line per triple

No prefixes, no shortcuts: easy to stream and split, but verbose.

3 · compression

Such repetitive text compresses very well with gzip.

4 · in a triplestore

Indexes add space; the real size depends on the engine.
